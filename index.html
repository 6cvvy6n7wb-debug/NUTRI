ABOUT NUTRI

What is NUTRI?

NUTRI is a web-based dietary and nutrient tracking system developed to help collect and organize information about respondents' food intake and water consumption.

The system provides estimated nutrient information, summarizes recorded dietary data, and generates research-oriented indicators based on the information entered by the respondents.

NUTRI is intended for academic research and data collection. It is not designed to diagnose diseases or replace professional medical assessment.

OUR RESEARCH

Supporting Dietary Data Collection

The NUTRI system supports the collection and organization of dietary information for the study. Recorded data may be used to examine selected dietary patterns and research indicators among respondents.

The system's generated scores are experimental research indicators and are not clinically validated medical risk assessments.
