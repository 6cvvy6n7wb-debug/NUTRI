About NUTRI

NUTRI stands for Nutrient Tracker. It is a research prototype developed to collect and organize dietary and water-intake information among Senior High School students. The system summarizes recorded data and generates an experimental risk-inference score. Its output is not a medical diagnosis, and its scoring method has not been clinically validated.

About the Research

This project explores how a nutrient tracking system can organize dietary and water-intake data for research purposes. The results should be interpreted cautiously and must not be used to diagnose or predict a urinary tract infection. Consult a qualified health professional for medical concerns.
